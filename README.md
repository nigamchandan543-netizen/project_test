# PR. 2 Analyzer — Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview
**PR. 2 Analyzer** is an Excel-based sales analysis project built to explore order-level sales data, understand customer and regional performance, track monthly growth, and present key business insights in a dashboard.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales, cost, and profit across regions and products.
- Compare customer sales and identify high-value customers.
- Track monthly sales, profit, order volume, and growth.
- Explore discount scenarios and their potential impact on profit.
- Summarize results with pivot-style tables, charts, statistics, and an executive dashboard.

## Workbook Structure

| Worksheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Sales Data` | Source order-level dataset, including date, customer, region, product, quantity, price, discount, sales, cost, and profit. |
| `Customer Analysis` | Customer sales/profit totals, order count, average order value, profit margin, ranking, and customer value flag. |
| `Monthly Growth` | Monthly sales, profit, order count, and sales growth. |
| `What-If Analysis` | Evaluates how discount changes may affect sales and profit. |
| `Stats & Regression` | Descriptive statistics and regression-related analysis. |
| `Pivot Analysis` | Region-by-product sales and profit summaries. |
| `Charts` | KPI visualization data and chart summaries. |
| `Dashboard` | Executive KPIs and headline findings. |

- **Top region by sales:** Central — $670,259.45.
- **Top product by sales:** Laptop — $1,067,033.96.
- **Sales leadership:** Laptop is the largest product category by revenue in this dataset.
- **Customer analysis:** the workbook ranks customers and flags high-value customers for further review.
- **Monthly performance:** the `Monthly Growth` sheet compares sales and profit month by month. Investigate months with sharp drops or spikes before making business decisions.

## How to Use
1. Open `PR2_Analyzer.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel or a compatible spreadsheet application.
2. Start with `Sales Data` to review the source records.
3. Explore `Customer Analysis`, `Monthly Growth`, `What-If Analysis`, `Stats & Regression`, and `Pivot Analysis`.
4. Use `Charts` and `Dashboard` for a quick visual summary.
5. If you edit the source data, check that formulas, summaries, and charts recalculate correctly.
